# Third-party data notices

## NASA POWER

Climate values are retrieved at runtime from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project Climatology API. The site requests temperature, corrected precipitation, all-sky surface shortwave downward irradiance, and relative humidity for January 1991 through December 2020.

- POWER: <https://power.larc.nasa.gov/>
- Referencing guidance: <https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/referencing/>
- NASA Earthdata data use policy: <https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software/data-use-policy>

NASA does not endorse this site. NASA names and identifiers are not used as product branding.

## Natural Earth

The world boundary layer is derived from Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 Countries. Natural Earth data is in the public domain.

- Source: <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/>
- Terms: <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>

Country boundaries are shown only as geographic context and do not express a legal position.
